# First-start feature card

The implementation is frozen at `0a8f82068ca623881cc58bdd4e10ba920f6fc4e0` on `frontend/whats-new`, based on master `9758260a`. The [design note](../design/whats-new.md) records its storage and navigation contract. The final headless browser run passed against this frozen source.

Startup creates one informational app notification with a fixed feature-bundle identity. Later starts reuse it. The same v1 record remains after dismissal or Clear All, outside ordinary age and count pruning, so it cannot return merely because history was pruned. There is no extra marker file, store migration or new page method. Creation and dismissal tests use isolated homes.

The card opens Decisions, Working with Chats, Settings at Background Chats and Settings at Set-aside Programs. Products advertising updates also get its button. Links navigate without answering questions, changing settings, removing files or restarting a program. Opening links leaves the card available. The card does not open its own window, emit a toast or add to the attention count.

The existing full pane preserves arrival order. A reserved scrollbar gutter keeps its earlier cards from changing width as content grows, and the feature card has a fixed-height scrolling body. Slow product discovery cannot delay existing notifications. A generation check keeps a delayed response from resurrecting a dismissed card. The Background Chats address is restored before window adoption and focuses its limit control only when Settings is in front.
