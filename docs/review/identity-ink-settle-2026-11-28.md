# Identity ink fixture correction

This correction starts at 654a4c7e, with PUDL 0.112.0 and the matching content distribution. The application and vendored files are unchanged. The only browser-driver change disables background images on the audit buttons. The equality predicate remains byte-for-byte unchanged; there is no pixel tolerance, extra timer or retry in the test.

The original check compares every pixel inside a glyph's bounding rectangle, including the background visible through transparent parts of its mask. Preserved failed screenshot pairs have identical computed foreground, mask and geometry, but differ by one channel unit in the gradient background. Empty regions beside those same glyphs show the same checkerboard differences even though they contain no glyph and their button state and geometry do not change. In the standard/light examples, those background-only regions contain 37, 118 and 115 differing pixels beside document-disabled, files-current and tools-current respectively. This establishes that the predicate was detecting background raster differences. It does not establish the renderer's internal cause.

The positive spatial controls use x=150..174 within the same audit button rows as the failing glyphs. The flat-substrate diagnostic has zero background differences in all 144 current/disabled control regions and passes all 433 assertions. The committed control summary retains representative raw RGB pairs and all nine failed observations. The original full logs and PNG pairs remain under pudl-0112-logs and pudl-0112-e2e-2 in the temporary directory.

Several unchanged-scene controls did not reproduce the difference. They include hiding and showing the same panel, cloning the same buttons, removing and restoring the same gradient, repainting a temporary background patch, and removing and restoring fallback-only metadata. These are negative evidence; the correction does not claim that every repaint changes gradient bytes. Their separate receipts remain under identity-ink-repaint-control, identity-ink-clone-control, identity-ink-gradient-repaint-control, identity-ink-patch-repaint-control and identity-ink-fallback-roundtrip-control in the temporary directory. An initial diagnostic failed because its output directory was absent. Its verified owned process trees were stopped, and the driver was corrected before the controls were run.

The revised test still checks actual masked paint under each admitted skin's foreground and native current/disabled treatment, with identical bounds and an exact pixel predicate. It no longer covers ink against a skin's real gradient background. The separate identity-kinds suite continues to compare the real host layout with and without identity attributes. The earlier single passing paint runs and frame waits did not establish repeat stability and do not override the preserved failures.

The consecutive-run proof uses one unchanged test-source hash and separate output directories under identity-ink-settle-proof in the temporary directory. Its results.json records every run's exit code, assertion counts and source hash. No failed attempt is omitted or retried inside that proof.

All ten consecutive runs pass 433 of 433 ink assertions, with exit zero and no page errors. The first run also passes identity-kinds at 121 of 121. The unchanged ink-driver SHA-256 is e2a2ebc5bc717a5a2819f155d057d9aa90ed36c187effc53e65c4db64db5637d. The exact pixel predicate was compared against the base source and is unchanged. The shared browser queue was used for every run.
