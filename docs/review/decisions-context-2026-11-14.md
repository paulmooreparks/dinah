# Conversation context in Decisions

The candidate is on `frontend/decisions-context` in `.worktrees/decisions-context`. Frontend implementation commit `c1334c5d` adds the fixed conversation area and its suite. Merge `93d5f842` brings in master `824054b3`, including Engine's bounded query from `13079771`. Commit `3cf491e6227d79b1fd9729cb0b8b3d328751ff29` makes membership events and messages without text readable. The final combined verification passed against this frozen source. The [design note](../design/decisions-context.md) records the presentation contract.

Group mentions and structured requests read up to five messages strictly before their carrying message, oldest first. Each entry has a sender, time, two collapsed lines, an expansion control and a link to the message in its group. The 18rem conversation area scrolls internally. Loading, failure, retry, empty results and expansion preserve its outer bounds. Direct requests explain that they have no group conversation.

The original BusGroupMessagesFrom call was unsuitable because it returns everything from its starting boundary through the newest message. Engine supplied BusGroupMessageContext with a bounded indexed selection and target-group validation. The page uses that query without marking anything read. Explicit group navigation follows the group's existing read behaviour. Late results from a previous selection are ignored. Invalid counts, IDs, ordering, groups or timestamps fail without drawing entries. Messages are rendered as literal text.
