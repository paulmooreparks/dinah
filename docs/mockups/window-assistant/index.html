    What is proposed

    • The assistant is opened from its item in the tray and with a key, and nowhere else. It is one popover of one size.
    • A request is typed in the person's own words, or pressed as one of the standing requests. A standing request that needs no judgement, such as tiling, is carried out with no model call.
    • The first version does four things: it arranges windows, it gathers what belongs together by meaning, it tidies, and it says what changed since the person last looked.
    • Its answer is the thing done, and one line that says what it did, with Undo. It never opens a window to answer.
    • "Since You Last Looked" is the morning line: at most three things, each a link to its window. It is in the assistant's own place and never over the person's work.
    • It can only do what the person could do by hand in the window. Closing is limited to chats that are idle and documents with nothing unsaved, and Tidy Up says what it left open and why.

    Try typing

    • "put the documents side by side and the chat under them"
    • "show everything about the payment work"
    • "close what is finished"
    • Anything else gets the answer a real request would get when the assistant cannot do it: what it can do, in one line.
