Reports toGroup
Every chat reports to a lead or to you.

Try: open a chat's ⋯ menu and choose Reports To… (a chat's own reports are not offered); open a member such as Gap 05 Reviewer and send a message, then open its lead's team room to see the copies; fold a team; switch the theme.

A chat cannot report to itself or to a chat that reports to it, so those are not offered.
