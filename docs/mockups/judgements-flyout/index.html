The Judgements flyout

Mockup, 19 November 2026. The buttons in the proposed flyout work.

What is wrong with today's flyout

  • It has no title and no way to close it by a button. PUDL's popover has a head with both.
  • Everything it says is a paragraph of help text. The state, the day's count, the latest call, the budget and the pool are five facts written as run-on sentences, and the day's count is said twice.
  • Its categories are mixed. A raised button shares a line with the sentence it does not belong to, and the two ways out are rows of a list, drawn like a menu's, among paragraphs.
  • It keeps the size it opened with, and what arrives later is cut off. The day's line is read after the flyout opens, so it lands in a strip a few pixels tall with a scroll bar of its own. Your screenshot shows the last word of it, "now."
  • A warning is a home-made row. PUDL has a notice for exactly this, with its own colour, glyph and Dismiss button.

What I propose

  • PUDL's popover, whole: a head with the title and Close, the content, and the two ways out as buttons in its foot, with Open Judgements as the primary one.
  • The state as a glyph, a colour and one word (On, Warning, Paused, Off, Reading), which is how the status bar's own item shows it, and one line under it that says why when it is not simply on.
  • The five facts as a key-value table. Every row is there from the start, and a value that is still being read says so, so nothing arrives later to change the flyout's size.
  • Warnings as notices under their own heading, with Dismiss All beside the heading it belongs to. Only the warnings scroll.
  • Nothing moves. The flyout has one width and one height for as long as it is open. Dismissing a warning moves the ones below it up inside their region and moves nothing else. This also removes the script that measures the flyout as it opens and freezes its size.

Alternatives

  • Room for warnings. I recommend that the region always has room for two warnings, which costs about nine lines of empty space on a quiet day and means a warning that arrives while the flyout is open has somewhere to go. The other choice above gives the region one line when the flyout opens with no warnings, which is tidier on a quiet day. A warning that arrives while it is open is then seen one at a time in that line.
  • No warnings in the flyout at all, only a row "Warnings: 2" and the button to the Judgements page, where they would be dismissed. It is the smallest flyout. It takes away dismissing a warning from where you first see it, which was asked for before.
  • A menu in place of a popover, as the workspace's and the agent's status items have. Those are lists of commands with a line of state above them. This one is mostly facts with two commands, which is what a popover is for.
