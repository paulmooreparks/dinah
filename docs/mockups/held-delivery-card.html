What changes.

  • The title says what is wrong, and the body gives the count and when the opening failed.
  • A held opening gets Retry Opening, Open Chat and Dismiss. Holds of other kinds keep the buttons they have today.
  • Dismiss hides the card until another message is held. Today it stays hidden until the hold itself changes, however many messages pile up.
  • The hold ends by itself when the chat takes a turn. That needs no card and is being built now.

What the sender is told. When an agent sends to a held chat, its receipt now ends like this:

Sent as message 4121 to Parser Team Lead. Parser Team Lead has held messages: an earlier opening of it never finished, so this message waits until that chat takes a turn of its own or the hold is cleared in Notifications.
