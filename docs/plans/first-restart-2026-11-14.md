# Your first restart today

Your current window does not have Pause Chats and Restart. Use an ordinary close and start for this restart.

## Before you close

1. Wait for Dinah Team Lead to confirm that the builds are ready, including the protection for the previous program.
2. Save your open documents and wait for any save or publish to finish.
3. Read [Going back to yesterday's build](going-back-2026-11-14.md). Ask Dinah Team Lead to confirm that your way back is ready before you close the current window.
4. Wait for the team leads to confirm that they have paused their teams and that their chats have stopped working.

## Restart the programs

1. Close Dinah in the usual way, and any other product built on it that is open.
2. Start each program again from its Start menu entry or its installed program. Allow it to finish opening before checking the changes.

## After they open

1. Look for the Decisions count in the status bar. Open it to read any waiting requests and their recommendations. Old group messages you already answered should not fill the list.
2. Open Notifications. Attention cards should still be there for requests you have not opened, answered or dismissed. Several requests from one chat share a card with a count.
3. Tell the team leads that the programs have reopened so they can resume their teams. Automatic carry-on messages belong to chats paused through the new Pause Chats and Restart command. That command was not used this time, so do not expect those messages from this restart.

If a program does not open or something looks wrong, tell Dinah Team Lead before trying an older build. Follow the going-back note rather than starting the previous program on its own.
