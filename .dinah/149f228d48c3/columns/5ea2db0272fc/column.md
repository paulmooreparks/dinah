The board-wide entrance, standing outside every station. Cards land here raw: a title and a short framing description, often filed from a chat in two seconds. Bodies are optional, specs are explicitly NOT written here, and Intake is the only place on this board where unclassified work is allowed to exist.

Intake is an external bucket: no Ready/Active zone split, no WIP limit, no cycle-time clock. A card sitting here is captured intent rather than work in flight. Intake age measures capture-to-triage latency, and the flow clock starts at Triage.

### The only exit is Triage

Every card leaves this column into Triage and nowhere else. Triage is where a card's route through the board is chosen, and a card promoted from here to any other column has skipped that choice and taken the default route by accident, which is the longest one on the board. A card that got out another way goes back here rather than having its route guessed downstream.

### Who pulls

Any qualified agent, and the operator. A pull from Intake commits you to the triage glance and to nothing else: classify the card, choose its route, move it on. It is not a claim on the work itself, and the decision to spend a working station's budget on a card is made later, at the pull out of Design Queue or Build Queue.

Nothing is worked in this column. Classification happens in Triage, not here.

### When to leave a card here

Captured but not yet ripe: a feature concept whose user value is not clear, a bug report waiting on reproduction, a design question waiting for a workstream's direction. These are legitimate, and the board's filter surfaces them when the time comes.
