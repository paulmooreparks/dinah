A model proposed on 2026-08-24 says a card is never a piece of the product and is always a claim about what is not yet true, either knowledge that is missing or text the product does not yet carry. If that holds, a board cannot drift against its deliverable, because the two share no content and the board empties as the product fills in.

The claim is untested and it is the foundation the rest of the model stands on. This card tests it against boards that already exist rather than against a board built to demonstrate it.

Read every card on the incident workbench, the one-pager workbench and this board, and sort each into three piles: an absence of knowledge, an absence in the product, or neither. The third pile is the finding. A card that is a piece of structure, a container, or a place to hang attachments is evidence that the model is too clean, and how many such cards there are decides whether it needs adjusting or discarding.

This is an hour's reading against finished work and it should be run before anything is built on the model.
