Arabic is the second right-to-left language and it adds a problem Hebrew does not have, since its letters change shape depending on which letters sit beside them. That is a display concern on top of direction, and it is the kind of thing that looks correct in a file and wrong on a screen. The operator expects to find an Arabic reader; until one is available the catalog ships labelled as unchecked, which is the arrangement this project has chosen deliberately. This card carries the catalog itself.
