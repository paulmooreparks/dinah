If the chosen combination includes a refusal at claim time (lettered option A in the open question): a test constructs two distinct discriminators sharing one actor label, has the first claim the card, and asserts the second is refused with a message distinguishable from the message the matching-discriminator handoff case produces. A test that reuses one discriminator for both sides fails this criterion even if it passes, because it has not constructed the conflict case.
