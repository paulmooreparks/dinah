Two commands advertise that they remove a state, `archive` and `delete`, and no command anywhere in Dinah's surface adds one. A reader who wants a fourth column on their board finds nothing in the help block, nothing in any per-command page, and nothing in a guide that answers them; the layout guide says hand-editing the workbench is legal and shows the directory, without connecting that to the question they arrived with. An audit of every help surface recorded this as the largest gap between what the tool can do and what a reader can learn it can do. This card asks whether editing the flow is a command, a documented hand-edit, or both.
