The tool ships English and Hindi in full and stubs for six other languages, and nothing tells a person which of those a native speaker has actually read. The operator intends to ship translations before they are validated, state plainly which are validated and which are not, and invite contributors to improve them, so the honest label is what makes that plan safe rather than a quality claim nobody can stand behind. This card gives a language a validation status that lives with the catalog rather than in prose, surfaces it where a person looks, and opens the path from noticing a clumsy phrase to submitting a better one. Without the second half the invitation is a disclaimer rather than an invitation.
