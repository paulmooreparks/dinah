Asking to add a card without a title, in Hindi, produces a sentence that is Hindi except for the word "title" sitting in the middle of it in English. The catalog carries the sentence but the name of the thing that was missing arrives from elsewhere untranslated, so a Hindi reader meets an English noun in the middle of their own language. The refusal-composition work fixed this for one such slot and this is a different one, found while testing that card and outside its criteria. Hindi is the one language the tool ships complete, so it is the language in which a leak of this kind is most visible.
