The README's Status section tells a reader that no downloadable build exists yet and that the only way to get the tool is to build it from source. That sentence was true when the operator ratified the Status text, and it stops being true the moment the release workflow lands and cuts its first build. A person arriving from a download link would then read, on the same page, that the thing they just downloaded does not exist. The operator's approval of the Status wording already permits deleting a sentence that has gone stale, and this card is that deletion.
