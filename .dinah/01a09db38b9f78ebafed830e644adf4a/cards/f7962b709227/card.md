`dinah.unknown-path` answers fifteen raise sites with one sentence, `nothing in this workbench answers to {detail}`. Two of those sites name a path on the filesystem the reader typed, `dinah attach <ref> <file>` and `dinah init --from <source>`, and neither path was ever inside the workbench, so the sentence tells the reader something untrue about where Dinah looked.

dinah-102 gives those two sites their own next step and leaves the base sentence alone, because a second base entry chosen by a named value is a mechanism the refusal shape has only for an absent subject. This card asks what the sentence should say when the reader named a file, and what the shape needs in order to say it.
