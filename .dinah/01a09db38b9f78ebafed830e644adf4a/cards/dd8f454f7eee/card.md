The claim command declares a refusal for taking work in somebody else's name, and the help page prints it in the check order directly beneath the table telling a reader what they may type. No surface accepts a holder distinct from the actor asking, so the refusal can never fire and a reader who goes looking for the thing it guards against finds nothing to type. Two separate design reviews reached this independently while checking other work. The card covers deciding what the right answer is, whether the refusal goes, the surface grows, or the help stops printing it, and whether a check can hold the general property that every declared refusal is reachable from some invocation.
