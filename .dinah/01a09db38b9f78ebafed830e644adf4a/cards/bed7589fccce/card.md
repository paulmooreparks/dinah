Alka Vishwa at GK runs an eleven-step Devin workflow for resolving Jira tickets, from locating the repository through confirming before a Jira comment or a git commit, with each step today living as a separate skill file. This card turns that workflow into the first workbench definition authored by mapping someone else's real process, with her steps as states and her skill content as per-state instructions. It is both her deliverable and the protocol's first test against a workflow that nobody on the Andoneer side designed. Points where her steps resist the state model are findings for the core-profile card, not things to paper over.
