The MCP head builds the working agreement in Go, naming four rules that bind an owner rather than a tool, and the profile's own section 8 states the same four. A reader on the command line meets neither, which is how an agent finishes a card and leaves a claim behind. Two statements of one contract can drift, and a third written into a guide would make three. This card asks where the four rules should live so that one text reaches the MCP instructions, the command line, and the profile, and what the command-line reader sees.
