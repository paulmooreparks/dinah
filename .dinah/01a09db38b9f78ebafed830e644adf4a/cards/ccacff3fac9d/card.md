Japanese and Chinese are where the operator most wants to find out how translation goes wrong, because each breaks something different. Japanese mixes three writing systems inside single strings, Chinese offers text with no spaces to break a line on, and shipping Chinese in both its written forms tests whether the catalog handles two variants of one language at all. All three read left to right, so they need no new display decisions, only the column-width work already underway. They ship labelled as unchecked until a reader is found for each.
