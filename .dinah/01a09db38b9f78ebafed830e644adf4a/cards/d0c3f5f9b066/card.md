The operator's standing ambition since joining GK is to capture the entire GK delivery process in one governable shape, and the closest existing approach is the aoy concept board on Andoneer. This card pursues that as a yokoten workbench: the honest method states joined with the ceremony overlay the process imposes, building on the Jira-wrapper shape (yokoten-8) and on the concept-pipeline method distilled from the operator's consultant skills. Its users are the GK consultant team, and its test is whether a process nobody can hold in their head becomes something a new consultant works correctly on day one because the bench serves the right instructions at the right station. It is deliberately downstream of the first external tester's workbench and the wrapper card.
