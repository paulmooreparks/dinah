title: A configured workbench either wins or says why it did not
column: 5ea2db0272fc
state: ready

---
Naming a workbench in your own settings is accepted without complaint and then silently ignored whenever you happen to be standing inside a different workbench. A person who set the value deliberately gets no signal that it was overruled, and nothing in the settings listing distinguishes a value that is in force from one that is being shadowed. Either the configured value should win, or the tool should say which workbench it actually used and why, and the settings listing should be honest about which rung of the ladder is answering.
