There is no one-line summary of what happens at a state, so a reader listing the flow sees only names and has to open a station to learn what it is for. On the customer board that raised this, that one-liner had a real job: it is what somebody sees before deciding whether a card belongs there. The board's author wrote the summaries into a table in a separate document because the format offers nowhere to put them. This is one of three proposals to let a state declare something about itself.
