Every field of every kind round-trips through `set` then `get`, at both heads, and the sample table and `bench.FieldsOf` agree in both directions. `cmd/dinah/field_sweep_test.go`, `TestEveryFieldOfEveryKindRoundTripsAtBothHeads`: the test carries `fieldSamples`, a table keyed by kind and field naming a legal value and a second legal value for each; it builds one workbench holding one of every kind in `bench.EntityKinds()`, including an attachment below the workbench, a column, a card and a comment; for every kind and every name in `bench.FieldsOf(kind)` it runs `dinah set <ref> <field> <sample>` and then `dinah get <ref> <field>` and asserts exit 0 and that the printed value is the sample, then repeats the pair through the `set_field` and `get_field` tools and asserts the tool's `value` member equals the same sample. It fails on a name `FieldsOf` carries that `fieldSamples` has no entry for, naming kind and field, and fails on an entry of `fieldSamples` naming a field `FieldsOf` does not carry. It is fatal when the pair count is zero.
