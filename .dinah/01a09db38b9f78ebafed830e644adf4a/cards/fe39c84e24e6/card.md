Judging movement rather than work requires knowing what moved, and nothing records that today. The journal says a card changed state, who held it and when, and says nothing about what the product did as a result.

The proposal is one line per completed piece of work, written before it is judged, naming what it moved in the product. Everything else in the model depends on it, since convergence needs how much changed, ripple detection needs what an answer touched, and judgement needs to know what it is judging.

This card tests whether that line is actually writable. Take several pieces of work that are already finished, from this board and from the GK one-pager trial, and write the line for each without special pleading. Then have somebody who did not do the work read only the line and say what changed.

Three outcomes are worth distinguishing. If the line is easy and useful, it becomes a convention in the state prose. If it is easy and useless, the model has a hole where its most important fact should be. If it is hard to write, the requirement is heavier than it looks, and the state prose has to teach it rather than assume it.

Do this on work whose product is a document as well as work whose product is code, because a diff is not available in the first case and the line has to carry the whole answer.
