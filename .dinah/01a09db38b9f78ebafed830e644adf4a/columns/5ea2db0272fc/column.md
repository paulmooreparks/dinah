The workbench's entrance, standing outside every station. Cards land here raw: a title and a short framing description, often filed from a chat in two seconds. Bodies are optional, specifications are not written here, and Intake is the only place on this workbench where unclassified work is allowed to exist.

A card sitting here is captured intent rather than work in flight. Intake age measures how long capture waits for triage, and the clock on the work itself starts at Triage.

### The only exit is Triage

Every card leaves this column into Triage and nowhere else. Triage is where the card is classified, and a card moved from here to any other column has skipped that classification rather than been exempted from it. A card that got out another way comes back here rather than having its classification guessed downstream.

### Who takes a card from here

Any agent, and the operator. Taking a card from Intake commits you to the triage glance and to nothing else. It is not a claim on the work itself, and the decision to spend a working station's budget on a card is made later, when the card is taken out of Design Queue or Build Queue.

Nothing is worked in this column. Classification happens in Triage.

### When to leave a card here

Captured but not yet ripe: a feature concept whose value is not clear, a defect report waiting on a reproduction, a design question waiting on a workstream's direction. These are legitimate, and `dinah ls intake` surfaces them when the time comes.

### This column holds nothing

Nothing is settled here and nothing has to be settled before a card arrives, so this column declares no hold and a card passes through it freely in both directions.
